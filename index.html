About the series

Love Songs blends traditional filmmaking with the language of TikTok: one-minute episodes, a 9:16 frame, and storylines built around the platform's trending sounds.

Created by Hayley Adams, Alyce Adams and Michelle Melky. The trailer gained 2.9 million views and the account 97K followers overnight. Season 2 was released in partnership with Tinder.
